## Required evidence packet

The release owner should retain the commit SHA, validation logs, Playwright HTML report and traces, staging resource
inventory before and after cleanup, deployment version/readback, rollback-drill record, incident-drill record, dashboard
and alert links, approved legal document versions, and the final signed go/no-go decision. Do not place credentials,
storage-state files, prompts, generated code, raw tool output, or customer identifiers in the packet.
